    What-to-wear weather app: live conditions, character avatars, timeline, fashion show
    
    Single-file index.html pulling live weather from Open-Meteo, mapping feels-like
    temp + precipitation + wind to an illustrated outfit recommendation via
    avatars/outfit-guide.json. Includes a day-stepper timeline (past 7 / today /
    forecast), rain and wind avatar variants, Halloween and 80s easter eggs, and a
    runway "Fashion Show" mode that cycles the full wardrobe to music.
